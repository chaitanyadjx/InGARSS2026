Visa Information

VISA is requested to all participants coming from Overseas.


Updated Procedure for e-Business Visa (e-B5V)

Please find the updated procedure for obtaining the e-Business Visa (e-B5V) for attending ACRS 2026 in India. Kindly note that the earlier e-Conference Visa has now been revised to the e-Business Visa category. All international attendees are requested to carefully follow the updated guidelines and apply under the new visa category.


Important Information

Applicants are advised to apply under the e-Business Visa (e-B5V) category, as shown in the screenshot below for your kind reference.




Required Information

The following information is required from all international delegates:

  • Name as per passport
  • Nationality
  • Place of Birth
  • Passport Number
  • Current Affiliation and Designation
  • Scanned copy of the Passport
  • Short CV

The conference organizer will apply for ministry approvals after collecting the above information. Kindly connect with Mr. Vikram Sharma at +91 9650422188 to submit your details. The time limit to provide the above information is 3 months prior to the conference dates.

We will apply for ministry approval after collecting the above information from you.


Documents Required for e-Business Visa (e-B5V)

Kindly apply for the e-Business Visa only once you have all these three documents. The documents will be available a couple of months prior to the conference dates.

  • Letter of Invitation
  • Ministry of Home Affairs Permission
  • Ministry of External Affairs Permission

To get the above documents, please send your request to the secretariat email ID: secretariat@acrs2026.in .


Note

  • The Ministry of External Affairs approval letter will be shared with registered attendees on their registered email IDs, and MEA will forward these to the Indian Embassy / Missions at each location.
  • Kindly apply for the e-Business Visa (e-B5V) only when all the above documents are available with you. Ministry permissions are ideally received 2 months prior to the conference dates.
  • Please make sure there are enough blank pages available in passports for immigration stamping. The passport should have a minimum validity of six (06) months from the date of travel/application, which is mandatory for visa processing and international travel.

Arrival Requirements for International Passengers

India e-Arrival Card

Please be advised that all foreign passengers, including OCI cardholders, are required to register for the India e-Arrival Card. Registration can be completed up to 72 hours prior to arrival.

This is a mandatory Indian Immigration procedure intended to ensure a smooth and efficient immigration process. Please complete the registration using the official e-Arrival Card website:

https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/earrival/

Air Suvidha Portal for International Passengers

The Air Suvidha Portal has been activated for international passengers in connection with Ebola surveillance.

  • Passengers must complete the self-declaration form on the Air Suvidha portal at least 24 hours before arriving in India.
  • Passengers must download the completed form and carry it with them for presentation at the immigration counter.

Please complete the form through the following portal:

https://airsuvidha.civilaviation.gov.in

These procedures are recommended to help ensure a smooth process upon arrival in India.
