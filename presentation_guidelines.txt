IEEE InGARSS 2026: Rules and Guidelines for Authors, Presenters and Participants
Even
t: IEEE India Geoscience and Remote Sensing Symposium (InGARSS 2026)
Dates and venue: 01–04 December 2026, The Park, Hyderabad, Telangana, India
1. General Rules for All Attendees
1.Every participant, including authors, co-authors, speakers, students, delegates and accompanying persons, must complete the applicable registration before entering any session.
2.You may be asked to show valid student or IEEE membership proof. The documents required for each category are listed in the Important Notices on the first page.
3.Collect your badge and conference kit at the registration desk. Please arrive early to avoid queues. Registration timings are 8:30 AM to 9:30 AM and 2:30 PM to 4:30 PM each day, as stated in the Important Notices on the first page; any change will be announced on the conference website and by email.
4.Bring your registration confirmation email and a valid government-issued or institutional photo ID for verification.
5.Wear your official badge visibly at all times at the venue. Badges are non-transferable and may be checked by volunteers or security staff.
6.Entry to technical sessions, workshops, tutorials, lunches and special events depends on your registration category and the permissions for each event.
7.Arrive at least 10 minutes before each session begins, and stay until it ends. Avoid late entry, especially during technical presentations and keynote addresses.
8.Treat speakers, session chairs, volunteers and fellow participants with respect. Harassment, discrimination, abusive behaviour and deliberate disruption will not be tolerated.
9.Keep mobile phones on silent mode inside session halls.
10.Photography, audio or video recording and live streaming of presentations need the presenter’s permission and the organisers’ approval. Respect unpublished research and intellectual property.
11.Eat and drink only in the designated areas, and follow the venue’s safety and housekeeping rules. Please keep the venue clean.
12.Follow the official conference programme. The organising committee may change session timings, room allocations or arrangements when necessary.
13.Participation and presentation certificates are issued according to the eligibility.
14.Follow the IEEE Code of Ethics and IEEE conference conduct policies.
15.Comply with the venue’s security requirements and with instructions from security staff, volunteers and the organising committee.
16.You are responsible for the safety of your belongings. The conference is not responsible for any loss.
2. Oral Paper Presentations
1.Check the final technical programme for your presentation date, time and session room.
2.An author must present the paper in person at the venue. Virtual or pre-recorded presentations are allowed only with specific authorisation from the organising committee.
3.Report to your session hall at least 15 minutes before the session starts and introduce yourself to the session chair.
4.Each paper has 10 minutes for the presentation and 3 minutes for questions and answers, 13 minutes in total. Keep strictly to this time. The session chair may stop a presentation that overruns.
5.Prepare your slides using the conference template. Please follow the template strictly.
6.Submission. Submit or upload your presentation file by the deadline announced by the technical programme committee, following the organisers’ instructions.
7.Carry a backup of your presentation on a USB drive. A PDF backup is also recommended.
8.Use the conference-provided presentation system / laptop.
9.The presentation must match the accepted paper. Any substantial change to the title, authorship or technical content needs approval from the technical programme committee.
10.Stay available for the question-and-answer period and answer technical questions professionally. You are also encouraged to attend the whole session and take part in the discussions.
11.If the designated presenter cannot attend, another co-author may present with prior notice to, and approval from, the organising committee, subject to the registration requirements. Any change of presenter or presentation mode must be approved in advance.
12.If you do not present an accepted paper without prior authorisation, the paper may be marked as a no-show and may be excluded from IEEE Xplore (see Section 8). Please be at the session room well in advance of your slot.
3. Poster Presentations
1.Design the poster using the template on the conference website. Display your poster ID or paper number prominently so that visitors and evaluators can find it.
2.You are responsible for printing your poster and bringing it to the venue. However, the vendor details are provided in the conference website. 
3.Put up your poster in the designated area before the session begins, following the poster coordinators’ instructions. Use only the mounting materials the organisers permit, and avoid damaging boards or venue property.
4.Be ready to explain your work in about 3–5 minutes, followed by technical discussion and questions. At least one author must stay near the poster throughout the assigned period.
5.A poster displayed without an author present during the assigned session may be treated as not presented, subject to verification by the session chair or poster coordinator.
6.Poster papers are subject to the same registration, presentation, quality and publication requirements as other accepted papers.


For queries about registration, paper presentation, visas or anything else, please contact the official InGARSS 2026 email id ingarss2026@gmail.com.
